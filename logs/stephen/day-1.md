## Day 1 Reflection

### What happened
Participated in the Charter and Board workshop with the team, where we discussed the project requirements, roles, and sprint expectations. I took on the Backend Developer role, mainly focusing on API logic and how the backend will connect with the frontend.

We agreed to explore a simple rule-based approach for the MVP, focusing on areas such as order status and returns/refunds. I also reviewed the project requirements and got familiar with the repository, board, and GitHub workflow.

### What went well
- I was able to identify backend as the area where I can contribute best.
- The team was supportive, engaged, and willing to share ideas.
- Everyone took responsibility for their assigned roles.
- The rule-based approach gave us a clear and realistic starting point for the MVP.

### What was harder than expected
- Understanding how the backend, database, and frontend would eventually work together took some discussion.
- Getting familiar with the repository and GitHub workflow took some time.
- Turning the requirements into specific backend tasks required some planning.

### Self-assessment
By the end of Day 1, I had a clearer understanding of my role and what I needed to contribute. I feel confident about working on the backend, while also knowing I need to improve my API development, testing, and integration skills as the project progresses.
