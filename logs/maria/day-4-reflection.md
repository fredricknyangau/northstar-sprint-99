# Day 4 Reflection

## What Happened

Today I continued working on the Northstar project and reviewed the work completed so far with the team as we prepare to close out the current assignments.

Earlier in the day, I discovered a merge conflict in `frontend/script.js`. The issue was caused by a small mistake in my implementation where I had written `https` instead of `http` for the local backend API URL. Since the backend is running on `http://localhost:8000`, this caused an inconsistency during integration.

With Fredrick's help, I identified the cause, corrected the URL, and resolved the conflict. This helped me understand how a small configuration error can affect the integration between the frontend and backend and create problems when merging branches.

Later, we reviewed the work completed across the project and checked where everyone stood with their assigned tasks. The team also discussed the final review process, including confirming that board tasks accurately reflect the work completed, running a live demonstration of the backend and frontend together, and verifying the contribution information and PR links before submission.

I was able to resolve the merge conflict with Fredrick's guidance and understand what had caused it rather than simply fixing the conflict without understanding the problem.

The team is also in a good position to finalize the project. Reviewing the completed work together helped me see how my frontend contribution fits into the overall system and how the different team members' work connects during the final integration.

## Self-Assessment

I would rate my performance today at **8/10**. I made a small configuration mistake, but I was able to identify and correct it with guidance. I also participated in reviewing the overall project and preparing for the final submission.
